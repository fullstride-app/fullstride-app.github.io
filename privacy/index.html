Fullstride — Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Fullstride keeps your health data on your phone. This policy explains the little that leaves it, why, and what happens to it.

Who we are

Fullstride is published by INBOUND L.L.C, Office 59, Government of Dubai Property, Dubai, United Arab Emirates (“we”, “us”).

Questions about this policy or your data: fullstride.app@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days.

Fullstride has no account or login. We do not know your name, email, phone number or address unless you write to us.

What stays on your phone, and what leaves it

Everything you log is stored only on your phone. A few things are sent to our server, only when you use a feature that needs them.

Data Where it goes Why
Doses, symptoms, weight, water, fasting, supplements taken, lab values, targets, notes Stays on your phone Your log
Meal photos you take for reading Our server, then our AI provider To estimate what’s in the meal
Meals you type or say (speech is turned into text on your phone) Our server, then our AI provider Same
Supplement label photos Our server, then our AI provider To read the label’s amounts
Food names and amounts from the AI’s answer Our server Our server adds vitamins and minerals from US government food tables it holds itself (USDA FoodData Central); no outside database is contacted
A random app id created when you install Fullstride Our server To apply fair daily limits; it is not an advertising id and is not linked to you
Purchases Google Play Google handles payment; we receive only whether your subscription is active

Feedback you choose to send. If you use Send feedback or “Send this to support”, we receive your message, the app version, your phone model and Android version, and the results of the app’s own checks. This is sent only when you tap Send, after showing you exactly what goes, and is kept in Fullstride’s support records (a Google Sheet on Fullstride’s Google account) so we can reply and fix problems.

Nothing we send carries your name, contact details, doses or health history. We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools.

Backups. A backup file is created on your phone. It leaves the phone only if you choose to save or share it somewhere (for example Google Drive or WhatsApp); that copy is then under your control and that service’s terms.

Who handles the data we send

We do not keep your photos or meal descriptions. Our server passes them to our AI provider, returns the result to your phone, and discards them.

Who What they do How long they keep it
Our server (run on Cloudflare) Receives the request, passes it on, returns the answer Not stored; technical logs keep only counts and timings, never photos or text
Anthropic (Claude), our AI provider Reads the photo or description Anthropic automatically deletes API inputs and outputs within 30 days, longer only where needed to enforce its usage policy or comply with the law (Anthropic); API data is not used to train its models (Anthropic)
Google Play Handles subscriptions and payments Under Google’s own privacy policy

These providers act on our instructions. We never sell, rent or share your data with anyone else, including advertisers, insurers or employers.

Where it is processed. Requests may be processed outside your country, including in the United States. Because the data sent holds no name and no health history, and is not kept by us, Fullstride does not store health data outside the UAE.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your data: delete any entry in the app, or uninstall Fullstride to remove everything on the phone. We hold no copy to delete. Backups you saved elsewhere are yours to delete.
  • Use the app without sending anything: log meals by search, barcode or by hand instead of photo or AI reading.
  • Permissions: the camera (meal and label photos), microphone (speaking a meal) and notifications (reminders) are asked for only when you first use them, and can be turned off in your phone settings.
  • Ask us: email fullstride.app@gmail.com to ask what we hold (in practice, nothing linked to you) or to raise any concern.
  • Adults only: Fullstride is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone younger.
  • No ads and no selling: Fullstride shows no ads and never sells your data, even anonymised.

Health notice and changes

Fullstride is a tracker, not medical advice. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition, and it never calculates or recommends a dose. Always follow your doctor’s instructions.

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top, and tell you in the app when the change matters.
